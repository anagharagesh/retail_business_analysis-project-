
# Retail Business Performance & Profitability Analysis

##  Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data to understand business performance, profitability, product performance, regional performance, and seasonal sales trends.

The analysis was completed as part of a Data Analyst internship project.

##  Objective

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze overall retail sales and profit.
- Identify profitable and loss-making categories and sub-categories.
- Analyze regional and customer-segment performance.
- Identify slow-moving products based on quantity sold.
- Study monthly and quarterly sales and profit trends.
- Calculate profit margins.
- Create an interactive Tableau dashboard for visualization.

##  Key Findings

- Total Sales: **12,642,905**
- Total Profit: **1,469,034.82**
- Overall Profit Margin: **11.62%**
- Technology generated the highest sales and profit among the three main categories.
- Tables was the loss-making sub-category, with a profit of approximately **-64,083.39**.
- Q4 recorded the highest sales and profit among the four quarters.
- Sales and profit increased over the years from 2011 to 2014.
- Consumer segment generated the highest sales and profit.

##  Project Limitation

The dataset does not contain actual inventory-level or inventory-days information. Therefore, inventory days and overstocking were not calculated or artificially created.

Low quantity sold was used only as an indicator of slow-moving products.

##  Conclusion

The analysis provides an overview of retail business performance and identifies important areas related to profitability, products, regions, customer segments, and seasonal trends.

The Tableau dashboard makes the results easier to understand through interactive visualizations and filters.
